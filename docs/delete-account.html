How to request deletion

You can ask us to delete your account and the information associated with it at any time. There is no fee and we do not require you to give a reason.

  1. Email us from the same address or phone-linked account you used, or from any address, including if you no longer have access to the account. Use the subject line Delete my account.
  2. Include the mobile number you signed up with, in the form 8801XXXXXXXXX — for example 8801712345678. This is how we find your account.
  3. Send it to r11137307@gmail.com
  4. Wait for confirmation. We will email you to confirm once the deletion is done, normally within 7 days.

If you would rather not email, reply to any message from us or contact us through the Association using the details below. We will accept the request by any means you can manage.

If you cannot access the account any more, that is not a problem. Tell us the mobile number you signed up with and we will verify and delete it. You do not need the password.

What gets deleted

When we process your request, we permanently delete:

  • Your account and sign-in credentials
  • Your entry in the alumni directory, including your name, phone number, batch year, addresses, current activity, position and any blood group you chose to share
  • Your profile photo, if you had uploaded one
  • Any photos you uploaded to the shared gallery
  • Your administrator rights, if you had any

What we keep, and why

We keep a minimal record that your account was deleted, so we can identify and block anyone trying to re-register from your details in a way that would mislead other members. This record contains only the deletion request date and the hashed form of the mobile number. It cannot be used to contact you or to sign in.

We may also keep information where we are legally required to do so, for example under an applicable data protection law or a valid legal request.

Backups

Deletion from the live database happens as described above. Because we take routine backups, a residual copy of your data may remain in encrypted backup storage for up to 90 days before it is overwritten. That copy is not used for any purpose and is not accessible to anyone.

Effects of deleting your account

This cannot be undone. After deletion:

  • Your directory entry disappears from the app
  • You will not be able to sign in again with the old credentials
  • You are welcome to sign up again later with a new account

Deleting just your directory entry

If you want to keep your account but remove only your public entry, open your own profile in the app and delete the entry there. Blood group sharing is separate: un-tick the consent box on your entry and save to withdraw it from the directory.
