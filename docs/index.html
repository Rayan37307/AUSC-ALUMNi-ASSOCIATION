Privacy Policy for the AUSC Alumni Association App

Alumni Association of Aftab Uddin School and College, Kishoreganj, Bangladesh

Last updated:07/10/2026

1. Introduction

The AUSC Alumni Association app ("the App") is operated by the Alumni Association of Aftab Uddin School and College, Kishoreganj, Bangladesh ("the Association", "we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information the App collects, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

2. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account or sign in, the App collects:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • Your password, stored only in encrypted form by our authentication provider. We never see or store your password in readable form.
  • Your full name

Technical note. The App uses a phone number and password. For technical reasons each account is registered internally with an automatically generated identifier derived from your phone number. No email is ever sent to you.

Alumni directory information

When you add or edit an entry in the alumni directory, the App collects the information you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Batch year
  • Current address
  • Permanent address
  • What you are currently doing, such as your occupation or studies
  • Position or designation
  • Blood group, only if you separately choose to share it
  • Profile photo, only if you choose to add one

Photographs

The App can read photos from your device when you choose a picture, using the system photo picker. Photos you upload to the App's gallery are stored with the Association and may be visible to other signed-in members.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display and maintain the alumni directory
  • To show alumni photographs in the shared gallery
  • To allow other members to contact alumni who have chosen to share their phone number
  • To let members see who may be willing to donate blood, but only where the member has chosen to share their blood group
  • To respond to support requests

4. Who your information is shared with

  • Other signed-in members of the Association, for directory and gallery content you submit
  • Our service providers, who host the database and authentication systems and act on our instructions
  • Law enforcement or other authorities, only where we are legally required to disclose

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for advertising.

5. Data storage and security

Your information is stored on servers operated by our service providers and is transmitted over encrypted connections. Access to the App requires a signed-in account. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights

You may:

  • View your information and directory entry within the App
  • Correct or update your entry at any time
  • Withdraw consent to share your blood group at any time
  • Request deletion of your account and information
  • Ask what information we hold about you

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at r11137307@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable period.

7. Children's privacy

The App is intended for adults who were enrolled at Aftab Uddin School and College. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Photo permissions

The App requests access to your photos only when you choose an image to upload. You can deny this permission and the rest of the App will still work.

9. Third-party services

The App uses third-party services to host its database and authentication. Their handling of your information is governed by their own terms.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date. Continuing to use the App after a change means you accept the revised policy.
